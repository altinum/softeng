## Who invented spreadsheets?

In 1979, the two students invented **VisiCalc** after getting frustrated with homework in a business class, which led them to develop a radically new solution.

## What happened to VisiCalc? 

Meanwhile, Lotus 1-2-3 appeared and quickly took over VisiCalc's market position. In 1985, Lotus Development Corporation acquired Software Arts (and with it, the rights to VisiCalc), and almost immediately pulled the product from the market, since it would have competed with its own Lotus 1-2-3 software.

## Why excel? 

- In 1982 Lotus 1-2-3, an electronic spreadsheet that became the best-selling personal-computer software of its day. 
- Microsoft Excel was first released in **1985**, initially for the Apple Macintosh. The Windows version followed in **1987**.
- VBA (Visual Basic for Applications) was added to Excel in **1993**, with the release of **Excel 5.0**. This was also the version that introduced the modern tabbed worksheet interface. VBA replaced the older Excel 4.0 macro language and eventually became the standard automation language across all Microsoft Office applications.


**The chicken and egg problem** - How did Microsoft manage to completely push Lotus 1-2-3 out of the market and practically become the sole ruler in this area?
